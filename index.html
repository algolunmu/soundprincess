08 / DIAGNOSTICS + HARDWARE NOTES
Ready.

The NSX-39 has shared onboard effect algorithms, selected from the master console using Yamaha XG SysEx (02 01 00 and 02 01 20), with separate MIDI channel sends. CC91 reverb, CC93 chorus, CC94 variation send. Distortion and compression here are MIDI-level approximations, NOT independent audio processors. Yoko uses the Yamaha XG Variation Echo hardware algorithm (07 00) and sends CC94 on CH 1; instrumental MIDI echo rates are independent of master BPM. XG Echo delay time is limited to 355 ms. Hardware implementation may vary by NSX-39 mode. MIDI SysEx permission is required for type changes; support varies between NSX-39 operating modes. This is an experimental local Web MIDI app: verify device behavior and avoid MIDI loops.
